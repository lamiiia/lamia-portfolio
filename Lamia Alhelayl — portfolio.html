Mukhbir

01

AI-Generated Image Detection

Mukhbir is a gradient-based AI image detection system designed to distinguish real images from AI-generated ones. The project combines StyleGAN-based synthetic image generation with a Swin Transformer classifier, leveraging gradient-based image transformations to capture subtle visual patterns that help differentiate generated images from real photographs. I also contributed to building a curated dataset of 18,416 real images and 18,958 GAN-generated images across seven GAN models.
